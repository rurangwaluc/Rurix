# Rurix

Rurix is a Business Control System for product and service businesses.

Rurix helps business owners control:

- Customer requests
- Sales
- Payments
- Stock movement
- Service delivery
- Expenses
- Supplier bills
- Staff activity
- Branch performance
- Business risk

## Product principle

Every business action must have:

- A responsible person
- A status
- Proof where needed
- A next step
- A traceable activity history

## Locked operating model

Rurix does not require every business to have every staff role.

It assigns responsibilities to available people and keeps the workflow controlled regardless of team size.

## Business structure

- One business can have many branches.
- Every business must have one main branch.
- A business can have one primary owner.
- A business can have owners and partners.
- A business can have many staff members.
- A branch can have many users with the same role.
- A user can have multiple roles and responsibilities.
- A user can work in one or many branches if assigned.

## Authorization structure

- Roles are templates.
- Permissions control real access.
- Backend enforces permissions.
- UI adapts to assigned responsibilities.

## Offline model

Rurix is online-first, offline-capable, branch-aware, permission-first, and server-authoritative.

Allowed work can be stored offline and synced when internet is restored.
